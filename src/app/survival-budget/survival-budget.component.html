Calculation and pre-departure checks

Each leg = route meters ÷ (assumed speed × 1,000) × 60 + (metres climbed ÷ 100 × your climb minutes) + (metres descended ÷ 100 × your descent minutes). Route meters follow the ground when the accepted terrain model covers the route; otherwise the flat map distance is used. The return leg climbs what the outbound descended. Margin = available − reserve − tasks − both legs. Turn-back time = available − reserve − return leg.

Constant speed and the same outward/return path are assumed. This is a planning estimate, not a live countdown. Elevations come from a 1 m stereo terrain model with no stated accuracy; rocks, soil, obstacles and delays are unknown.
