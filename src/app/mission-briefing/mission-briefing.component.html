Scientific & Spatial Limits

  • Unlocated regional records: Science records have no verified pixel positions on this image frame (positionStatus: unavailable); exact spatial overlap is unverified. Do not assume Vera Rubin Ridge or Glen Torridon lies within this specific HiRISE frame.
  • Unavailable measurements: No verified metric scale, digital elevation model (DEM), slope, obstacle clearance, current route weather, or route safety rating is supplied.
  • Interpretation boundaries: Source findings do not establish groundwater presence today, unverified mineral weight percentages, or completion of rover measurements that source blogs describe as planned.

Rehearsal Workflow

  1. In Route Planner, place a Start point, optional Waypoints, and a Destination on the image.
  2. Open Confidence & Data to inspect a contextual science record and review its primary NASA source and limitations.
  3. Open Notebook to record an exploration observation distinguishing visual features from regional context.
  4. Use Save Route in Route Planner, prepare offline caching via the existing PWA controls when needed, reopen and explicitly click Load Route to recover saved work, or use Mission Transfer to export versioned JSON.
